KeepADB helps keep Wifi-ADB, Android’s “Wireless debugging”, available across device restarts, Wi-Fi reconnects, access-point changes, and idle periods.

Toggle Wifi-ADB from the main screen, a Quick Settings tile, or a home-screen widget. An optional foreground service monitors the connection and attempts recovery when Android disables it. Android may ask you to approve Wifi-ADB on Wi-Fi; approve only expected prompts on a network you trust. “Always allow on this network” lets Android enable Wifi-ADB automatically when you reconnect. KeepADB cannot grant that approval itself, and its trusted-network rule only controls where automatic recovery may run. If Android does not confirm an automatic enable, KeepADB backs off instead of retrying rapidly.

Features:
* Quick Settings tile, main app controls, and launcher shortcuts (Toggle Wifi-ADB, Wifi-ADB with timer, Wi-Fi networks, Settings)
* Wifi-ADB timer: on for 15 min to 24 h, then off again; countdown, “Off now” and “+1 h” in the notification
* Tap the endpoint to copy the adb connect command; “Connect a computer…” and the setup assistant explain how to pair a new computer
* Two home-screen widgets: KeepADB controls grows with its size; KeepADB overview shows the endpoint, Wi-Fi, Keep-Alive and webhook delivery at a glance
* Optional Keep-Alive service across restarts and network changes
* Endpoint discovery through Android NSD/mDNS (_adb-tls-connect._tcp), with a fallback when mDNS stops announcing the port; timing depends on network conditions
* Optional HTTP(S) webhook, disabled by default, for verified Wifi-ADB endpoints, with opt-in periodic refresh (15 min to 23 h 59 min)
* Optional automation interface, off by default and token-protected, plus a Tasker/Locale plugin; works with Tasker, MacroDroid, Automate and adb
* Configurable status notification and endpoint display
* Four optional privacy switches that hide names and addresses in the app, in notifications, and in widgets and the tile, and keep notifications off the lock screen
* Protection page: choose where Keep-Alive may re-enable Wifi-ADB, with a Wi-Fi networks list to trust, block, and name access points; “Turn off on open Wi-Fi” for insecure networks and captive portals; optional Force mode with a red notification, for a chosen time or until you end it
* Settings backup to a file you choose, without storage permission; the automation token is never included
* 19 interface languages and right-to-left layout support
* No advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party runtime libraries

On fresh installs, Keep-Alive re-enables Wifi-ADB automatically only at access points you have trusted; existing installations keep their previous network setting. Recognizing trusted networks requires precise Android location access, which KeepADB uses only to identify the Wi-Fi network; it does not read or store your location. If location is approximate or off, or Keep-Alive was started only in the background, recovery can pause until you open the app or allow location “all the time”.

KeepADB does not require root. Android protects the Wifi-ADB setting with the WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS permission, so setup grants it once: directly on the phone via self-pairing in the setup assistant (Android 11+, no computer needed), in a computer browser at https://keepadb.roteson.de/setup (Chrome/Edge over WebUSB), or manually over USB with ADB:

adb shell pm grant de.hohnepeople.keepadb android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS

Wifi-ADB exposes an authenticated development service on the connected network. Use Keep-Alive on networks you trust, turn Wifi-ADB off on public hotspots, and reject unexpected Android approval or pairing prompts.
