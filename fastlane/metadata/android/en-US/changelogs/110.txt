Changed: on first launch, KeepADB no longer pops the notification permission dialog out of the
blue. The home screen now explains why notifications help (background service, Keep-Alive and
connection status at a glance) and only asks -- or sends you to notification settings after a
denial -- when you tap the button.

Added: the battery-optimization advice on the home screen can now be dismissed with a close
button if you don't want to disable battery optimization. Re-enable it anytime from Settings.
