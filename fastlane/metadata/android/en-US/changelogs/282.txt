New: export and import your settings under Settings → Settings backup, e.g. before a reinstall. You pick the file; nothing is uploaded, and the webhook URL can be left out. An import never turns Wifi-ADB or Keep-Alive on. New: "Turn off on open Wi-Fi". While Keep-Alive runs, Wifi-ADB goes off in a Wi-Fi without secure password protection or behind a sign-in page. On for new installations, off for existing ones (Settings → Protection). Force mode overrides it.
