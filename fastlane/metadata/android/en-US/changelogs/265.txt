The USB-ADB area is gone: notification, host profiles and automatic handover. Keep-Alive is the way to have Wireless Debugging switched on again unattended. The webhook retry options are gone; the standard series is fixed. Launcher shortcuts: toggle Wifi-ADB, Wi-Fi networks, Settings; hold the tile to open KeepADB.
