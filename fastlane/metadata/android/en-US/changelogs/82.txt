A link-local IPv6 scope mismatch could silently fall back to a less strict comparison when the
device's own network interface could not be resolved, quietly reopening a spoofing gap that was
meant to be closed. This is now caught and made visible, and the device's own interface is
resolved more reliably. Stopping an active quick-probe scan now takes effect noticeably sooner.
The pending webhook cleanup backlog now discards the oldest entry instead of the newest one when
it overflows, and remembers the discard order across app restarts. The periodic health check of
an already-registered endpoint now verifies it still looks like the real wireless-debugging
service, not just that something answers on that port.
