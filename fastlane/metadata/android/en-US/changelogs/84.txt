Fixed a rare edge case in the optional USB-to-WLAN wireless-debugging handover: if you switched
its mode from Automatic to Off in the brief moment right after plugging in USB, the automatic
enable could still go through once on a network you had marked as trusted. It now checks the
current mode again right before switching wireless debugging on, so turning it off during that
short window reliably cancels the pending enable.

Also includes internal cleanup and test hardening: removed unused constant definitions that were
never read, added a test that locks in the exact internal step order the app already uses to keep
its network-trust checks race-free, and fixed a shared test-only safeguard that now resets an
internal test-only network tracker before and after every automated test, closing a gap that
could occasionally make an unrelated automated test fail depending on test order. No user-visible
behavior change beyond the USB handover fix above.
