Allowed access points are easier to tell apart: each one now has its own number that stays the same when other entries come or go, you can give it a name of your own with the small pencil, and the Wi-Fi band (2.4, 5 or 6 GHz, or "Band unknown") is shown behind its address. The band only uses what Android already knows; KeepADB starts no Wi-Fi scans. Which networks are allowed does not change.
