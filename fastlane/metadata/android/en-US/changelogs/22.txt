Fixed the Quick Settings Tile misreporting "Disconnected" while Wireless Debugging is on or Keep-Alive is still searching for an endpoint. The tile now shows a "Searching…" state instead, tapping it in that state retries discovery/reconnect instead of turning Wireless Debugging off (including when Wireless Debugging is actually off and Keep-Alive is just waiting), and briefly opening/closing the Quick Settings panel no longer aborts an in-flight discovery.
