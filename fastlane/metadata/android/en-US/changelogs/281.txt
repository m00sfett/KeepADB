New: "Turn off on open Wi-Fi". While Keep-Alive runs, Wifi-ADB goes off and stays off in a Wi-Fi without a password or behind a sign-in page, even at trusted access points. On by default for new installations; existing installations keep it off until you switch it on under Settings → Protection. A session you turn on yourself stays on, and Force mode overrides the rule. Android 11 detects sign-in pages only.
