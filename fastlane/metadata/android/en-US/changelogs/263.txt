Hold the launcher icon for shortcuts: toggle Wifi-ADB, Wi-Fi networks, Settings. Hold the tile to open KeepADB. The gear menu has four entries. Two widgets: controls and overview. Privacy section with three switches; tap the eye to hide a screen, hold it for the switches. New installs show Wi-Fi names and addresses in notifications until that switch is on. Missed a Wi-Fi prompt? Wi-Fi networks lists it. Saving the webhook URL sends one report; withdrawals are retried.
