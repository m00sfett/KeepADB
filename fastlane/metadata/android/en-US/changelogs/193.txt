With "Observe access points" on, an access point that is out of range still shows the Wi-Fi band it was last seen on, for example 5 GHz. If no band is known, nothing is shown anymore instead of "Band unknown". Turning the option off deletes the saved bands.

The privacy eye is now also in the title bar of Settings and of the access point lists. Allowed access points read "Name (No.)", with the number behind the name.
