Keep-Alive's automatic re-enable now reliably retries after a readback-mismatch pause: the
foreground service's heartbeat re-checks on its own once the pause has elapsed, instead of only
reacting to reopening the app or a network change.

Retries now follow a clearer cadence: first retry after about 2 minutes, then at most every 5
minutes.

Added an optional, purely local Tailscale status line to the main screen. It only shows when
Tailscale is installed and never affects Wireless Debugging or Keep-Alive.

The connection screen now shows every currently verified ADB connection separately -- WLAN,
Tailscale/VPN and USB -- with the one you'd actually connect through clearly marked, instead of
only ever showing the WLAN address. An active VPN alone is never shown as a working ADB
connection unless it's actually confirmed reachable. USB now shows up as its own active
connection too, even without a network address.
