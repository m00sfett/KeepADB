New optional force mode for maximum convenience: for 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or no end time, Keep-Alive switches wireless debugging back on in every Wi-Fi, even blocked or unreadable ones. You confirm it after clear warnings, see a warning on the home screen and in the notification, and end it with one tap. When the time is up your previous protection level applies again and you get one notice. It keeps counting across restarts and app updates.
