The main switch now reflects only whether wireless debugging is really switched on in the Android system; it no longer shows "ON" while the setting is off and Keep-Alive is merely waiting to switch it back on. That wait is shown as its own line of text instead. Tile, widget and the switch in the app now react identically to a tap in the same situation, a switch-over that is still waiting out the internal delay is visible as such, and a failed switch-over is no longer always reported as a missing permission (issue #318).
