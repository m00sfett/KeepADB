The main switch now reflects only whether wireless debugging is really switched on in the Android system; it no longer shows "ON" while the setting is off and Keep-Alive is merely waiting to switch it back on. That wait is shown as its own line of text instead. Tile, widget and the switch in the app now agree on what a tap means, with one deliberate exception: while wireless debugging is on but no endpoint has been found yet, a tap on the tile starts a new search instead of switching off. A switch-over that is still waiting out the internal delay is visible as such and no longer stays on screen when it is dropped, and a failed switch-over is no longer always reported as a missing permission (issue #318).
