Saving a valid webhook URL in the settings now also switches on webhook sync if it was off. You no longer have to tap the switch separately afterwards.
When you turn off "Observe access points", KeepADB now asks whether it should also delete the list of access points it has seen so far. Choose "Yes, delete" to remove it, or "No, keep" to keep it for later. Closing the question without an answer keeps the list.
