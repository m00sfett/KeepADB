Two home-screen widgets: KeepADB controls (Wireless Debugging, Keep-Alive, webhook sync) and KeepADB overview (address, Wi-Fi, webhook delivery). Privacy section with three switches; the notification one has a lock-screen option. Tap the eye to hide or show a screen, hold it for the switches. New installs show Wi-Fi names and addresses in notifications until you turn that switch on. Saving the webhook URL sends one report. Webhook withdrawals are retried. Tidier settings menu with icons.
